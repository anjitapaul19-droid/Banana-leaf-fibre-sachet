# Banana Leaf Fibre Tri-Layer Composite Sachet

## About the Project
Our project explores the use of discarded banana leaves to develop a tri-layer composite sachet as a potential alternative to conventional plastic packaging.

## Problem
Banana leaves are often discarded after use, while single-use plastic packaging contributes to environmental pollution.

## Our Solution
We aim to convert banana leaf-based material into a useful packaging product and evaluate its practical performance.

## Objectives
- Make productive use of banana leaf waste.
- Develop a tri-layer composite sachet.
- Evaluate strength, flexibility, moisture resistance, and usability.
- Explore opportunities for sustainable packaging and local livelihoods.

## Project Status
Prototype development and testing are in progress. Testing results will be added when available.

## Materials and Method
The materials, preparation steps, layer composition, and fabrication process will be documented as the prototype develops.

## Testing
Planned tests include strength, flexibility, moisture resistance, and usability. Actual observations and results will be recorded here.

## Expected Impact
The project aims to promote agricultural waste utilisation and explore a more sustainable packaging option.
